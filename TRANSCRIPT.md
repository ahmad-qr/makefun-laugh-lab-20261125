Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-suroso-69-one-liner-laugh-lab-6ccf3aae

The line (109 characters, one line):

> I didn't eat your sock. I was investigating where all the single socks go, and the evidence is now inside me.

Why this line: it answers the prompt — the excuse a robot vacuum gives
after eating a sock — with a deadpan turn: the investigation's evidence is
inside the vacuum.

The program (`main.py`, stdlib only) writes exactly this line to
`/output/entry.txt` and asserts the gate itself: 1–200 characters, not only
white space. It is deterministic — the same bytes on every run.
